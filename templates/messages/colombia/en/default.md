## Where to get information

We want to accompany you on this journey of discovery of the OpenStreetMap world.
When you start, you may have many doubts and questions. We recommend you visit our [Welcome Mat](https://welcome.openstreetmap.org/what-is-openstreetmap/).

We recommend you visit the [beginner's guide](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Beginners%27_guide) page, which provides many tools to help you start this process.
We want to be with you while discovering the world of OpenStreetMap.

Our country has the [Colombia home page](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/ES:Colombia), where you can learn about what the community is doing. There, you will also find a [mapping guide](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/ES:Colombia/Gu%C3%ADa_para_mapear), which explains how we adapt the mapping to our territory.

If you have a different question, you can ask it in our Q&A platform, [Community](https://community.openstreetmap.org/), or in the [OSM community in Colombia Telegram](https://telegram.me/osmco) chat space.

So, what can you map now?
We recommend that you add elements near you, such as:
Restaurants, businesses, streets, sidewalks, chairs/benches are essentially items or places you know well and know where they are.
Also, if you find a business that is already mapped, please make sure it has the address on the map, the phone number, etc.
This way, you will be adding details to what already exists.
Remember: You are the expert in your area!
You will see how satisfying it is to fill the map around you with details.

Don't forget that you can count on the community, and ask them anything you want.
Like you, we all started mapping in OSM and want to accompany you on this learning path.

Happy Mapping!
